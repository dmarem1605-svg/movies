2 🎭 Guess the Film by Emoji!

Each student has a secret film. Look at the emojis and guess which film it is!

🎯 How to play: Each of you has a secret film with a set of emojis. Show your emojis to the group (but don't reveal the name!). The others must guess which film you have. Click on a card to reveal the answer.

💡 How to play: Click a film card, then click a genre box to place it. Or drag & drop! Try to place all 11 films.

5 🌟 Which film would you like to be in?

Choose your favourite film from the list and explain why. Use the helpful phrases!

🗣️ Useful phrases to start your answer:

  • If I could be in any film, I would choose...
  • I would love to be in... because...
  • The film I'd like to be in is... It's a... film.
  • I think it would be amazing / exciting / scary / funny to be in this film because...
